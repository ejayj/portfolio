Spotify Wiz

Nov 2023-Dec 2024

Spotify's Daily Drive is one of many unique playlists mixed specifically for the user. This playlist in particular takes the users' music profile and their own curated selection of podcasts to create a radio-esque experience for those who have long commutes and wish to listen to it all.

The issue lies in that what if you want to listen to a certain public playlist? Or choose one of your own? Or what if you want to chose your own news stations and podcasts? That's where Spotify Wiz steps in.

Spotify Wiz allows users to choose their playlists, choose their favorite podcasts, and it handles the rest. With the same exact experience as Spotify's Daily Drive, Spotify Wiz will update your podcasts hourly, re-shuffle your playlists daily, and much more!

The website is not live as I am still currently working on some features, however the core functionality works and is up and running!

Overwatch Balancer

Nov 2023-Feb 2024

The Overwatch Team Generator/Balancer is a small project, also made for my local discord gaming server, to help create well-balanced teams for the tournaments that we would often hold. One issue was often enough that team randomizers didn't take into account a player's rank, or preferred role.

This team generator takes the average of the team's rank and displays it for the user to see. It also assigned roles randomly for all players if they don't choose one, and has a spot of rejects and configuration settings.

Once completed, the app will be able to see unbalanced teams and recreate a randomized team. The original code for this (which is in python) is able to do this, however along with the configuration settings for team building- the project never fully got re-written in javascript.

This project is live, however the fully front end UI has not been completed.

Course Scheduler - School Project

Fall 2022

This project is my first full coding project and my very first class project. The goal of the Course Scheduler is to replace the school's current registration and course scheduling software and simplify the process.

While it worked, it made planning a full semester course tremendously hard for students and professors, who had to enter most courses manually. This would often times lead to confusions, mistakes, double bookings for classrooms, and overlap between important classes in the same course level (i.e. Freshman need to take certain 100 level courses to move onto sophmore year, but these courses would be booked at the same time).

In this algorithm, Faculty and Department Chairs can list classes, their location, and what year a student will typically be when they take the class in order to make their experience easier. The algorithm will automatically create a class schedule for students and teachers. I was primarily front end however ended up going full stack to finish the calendar functionality as we had some group members drop out early on.
